# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People considering Samwise to overcome severe, untreated behavioural challenges that have resisted other treatment: screens addiction, porn addiction, social media addiction, need-for-approval / impulsive love-seeking behaviour, destructive relationships. They arrive privately, often after trying other things, often carrying shame. Many find Samwise through a clinician, a peer, or a quiet recommendation. They open the page on mobile late at night, or on a desktop in a rare moment of stillness. They are not casual visitors; they are at the bottom of a mountain.

Their job: decide whether Samwise is the companion that will stay with them through this. The single conversion is to schedule a Fit Assessment call.

## Product Purpose

The samwise-landing page is the first encounter with Samwise as an idea. Its job is not to sell, not to inform, not to convert by argument. Its job is to make the visitor feel that someone is already with them on this journey, before a single human conversation has happened. If the page does that, the call gets booked. If it doesn't, no amount of feature copy will compensate.

Success is the visitor reaching the Fit Assessment link with the feeling that they have already been seen.

## Brand Personality

Held, sacred, prepared.

- **Held**: the visitor should feel that the page is carrying them. Not assertive, not requiring them. Companion-first, not coach-first. Sam to Frodo, not Gandalf to Frodo.
- **Sacred**: the work the visitor is about to do is treated with reverence. The page does not joke, does not flirt, does not motivate. Behavioural collapse and recovery are framed as a sacred journey, not a wellness goal.
- **Prepared**: underneath the softness is unmistakable competence. The clinical advisor is named. The path is concrete. The visitor should feel that the people behind this know exactly what they are doing.

Voice is calm, plain, direct. No exclamation points. No marketing verbs. English only. The page never asks the visitor to "join us" or "get started"; it offers a hand and waits.

## Anti-references

This page must explicitly NOT resemble:

- **Mental-health-app aesthetic** (Headspace, Calm, Talkspace lane): teal and lavender palettes, soft rounded illustrations, emoji-faces, motivational micro-copy. Reads as juvenile and saccharine for the severity of the behavioural work.
- **SaaS-startup landing template**: hero metric, gradient mesh, isometric icons, identical feature-card grid, "Loved by 10,000 teams" social proof. The AI-slop reflex for any tech-adjacent landing page.
- **Crypto / brutalist-tech**: neon-on-black, monospace headlines, dense terminal-style layouts. Reads as cold, transactional, juvenile-tech.

## Design Principles

1. **The page is a scene, not a document.** The visitor enters a place, not a brochure. Composition (background, foreground, characters) is primary. Copy serves the scene, not the other way around.
2. **Suggest, never depict.** The Mount Doom journey is the design compass; visitors see a sacred-journey metaphor without explicit Tolkien references. Silhouette and light only. The reference is the writer's room, not the stage.
3. **Movement carries meaning.** Motion is not decoration; it is how the journey is told. Each beat (descent, fall, rescue, struggle, redemption, rest) is rendered through movement of the figures, the mountain, the star, the ring. If motion is removed, the scene must still be comprehensible, but the *story* is delivered through motion.
4. **Hold, don't sell.** The page never asserts itself, never urges, never promises. It offers presence first and a hand second. The Fit Assessment link is not a CTA; it is a hand extended at the moment the visitor is ready to take it.
5. **Sacred restraint.** Monochrome silhouette plus light. The star and the ring are the only luminous accents. Tinted near-white background, never pure white. No decoration that is not load-bearing on the scene.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast minimum across all text and meaningful graphics.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`: when set, all motion degrades to a quiet opacity-only fade per scene beat. Decorative motion (the star descending, the figures climbing) is replaced with a static rendering that still conveys the same beat. No content is delivered ONLY through motion.
- Mobile-first: 375px viewport with no horizontal overflow; tap targets at least 44px on the Fit Assessment hand-extension.
- All copy in English; reading age aimed at general adult audience.
